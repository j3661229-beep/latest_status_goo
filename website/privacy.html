Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 12 October 2026 • Compliant with DPDP Act, 2023 (India) • Domain: statusgo.in

Notice to Users: Status Go ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. We process personal data strictly in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 of India.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal data necessary to provide and personalize daily status posters:

  • Account Identification: Mobile number verified via SMS OTP, or Google profile details (name, email) if you choose Google Sign-In.
  • Personalization Information: Your display name, profile photo, and state/language preferences that you choose to overlay on status templates.
  • Optional Business Details: If you use business branding, your business name, logo, or public contact line.
  • Device & Diagnostics: Device model, OS version, and crash logs to improve app stability.
  • Transaction Metadata: For optional Pro subscriptions, payment IDs and timestamps generated by Razorpay. We never store credit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs.

2. What We Never Collect

We respect your device boundaries:

  • We do not access your address book or contact lists.
  • We do not scan or read other photos in your phone gallery beyond the single photo you select for your poster frame.
  • We do not sell your personal data to data brokers or third-party advertising networks.

3. Purpose of Processing

We process your data strictly to:

  • Generate your personalized festival posters and status graphics.
  • Deliver advance festival notifications and morning greeting reminders.
  • Enable customer support and troubleshoot errors.
  • Comply with applicable Indian statutory requirements.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

To run our secure infrastructure, we work with industry-standard, compliant providers:

  • Google Cloud Platform: Secure server hosting and encrypted database storage (Mumbai Region).
  • Razorpay: RBI-authorized payment gateway for processing UPI and card payments.
  • SMS Gateways (2Factor / Firebase): For authenticating login OTPs.
  • OneSignal: Delivering opted-in festival countdown notifications.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your profile information only for as long as your account remains active. You may request permanent deletion of your account and associated profile assets at any time by emailing support@statusgo.in. All personal data is deleted within 30 days of verified request.

6. Your Rights Under DPDP Act, 2023

As a data principal, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of your personal data processed by Status Go.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate data.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time through app settings.
  • File a grievance with our designated Grievance Officer.

7. Grievance Redressal Officer

In accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and DPDP Act, 2023, our designated Grievance Officer is:

Grievance Officer: Grievance Redressal Cell
Entity: Status Go (statusgo.in)
Email: grievance@statusgo.in
Support Desk: support@statusgo.in
Response Time: Within 48 business hours

8. Updates to this Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect platform features or regulatory changes. Any significant change will be highlighted with a revised effective date at the top of this document.
