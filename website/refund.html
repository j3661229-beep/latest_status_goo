Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: 12 October 2026 • Payment Gateway: Razorpay (India) • Domain: statusgo.in

Our Philosophy: We want you to be 100% happy with Status Go. All plans on our app are one-time prepaid purchases (no secret auto-renewals). If you face any issues, our refund process is simple and hassle-free.

1. 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you purchase an Annual Pro or Monthly Pro plan and find that the app does not meet your expectations or experiences technical issues that we cannot resolve, you are entitled to a full refund within 7 days of your purchase date.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, simply send an email to support@statusgo.in with:

  • Your registered mobile number or email ID.
  • Your Razorpay Payment ID or Order ID (sent via SMS/email at checkout).
  • A brief description of why you'd like a refund (to help us improve).

3. Processing Time & Mode

Once approved, your refund will be initiated immediately via Razorpay. The amount will be credited back to your original payment method (UPI, bank account, debit/credit card) within 5 to 7 business days, subject to your bank's standard settlement timelines.

4. Ineligible Refund Scenarios

Refunds cannot be granted in the following circumstances:

  • Requests submitted after 7 days from the transaction date.
  • Accounts that have been suspended or terminated for violating our Acceptable Use Policy (e.g. sharing prohibited, defamatory, or abusive content).
  • Transactions processed through unauthorized third-party resellers.

5. Cancellation & Non-Renewal

Because Status Go does not practice sneaky recurring credit card deductions, you never need to worry about being surprised by an unexpected renewal charge. When your paid access period expires, your account gracefully transitions back to the Free plan without interrupting your saved favorites.

6. Support Desk

For any billing or transaction assistance, please contact:

Billing Desk: support@statusgo.in
Grievance Officer: grievance@statusgo.in
Official Website: statusgo.in
