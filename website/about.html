🇮🇳 About Us

Made in India,
for India

Status Go is India's leading WhatsApp Status template platform — helping millions of Indians share beautiful, personalised greetings every single day.

🚀

Our Story

Status Go was born from a simple idea: every Indian deserves a beautiful, personalised way to celebrate festivals and connect with loved ones — without needing design skills.

We started with a small library of festival templates and a vision to make every WhatsApp status special. Today, with over 10 lakh downloads and a growing library of 500+ templates, we're proud to be the go-to status app for millions across India.

Our app supports Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati and English — because India's diversity is its strength.

🎯

Our Mission

To make every Indian feel proud and connected — one beautiful status at a time.

We believe that sharing a warm greeting on Diwali, sending a good morning wish, or celebrating Eid with a personalised poster is a small but meaningful act of love. We make that effortless.

We are committed to building an app that is fast, beautiful, private and truly Indian — with fair pricing and no dark patterns.
